# getting better sleep

Entry 1
U: I have been sleeping badly this week. Do you have any advice?
A: Going to bed at the same time each night helps many people. Do you keep regular hours?
U: Not really. Some nights it is ten, other nights well past midnight.
A: That could be part of it. You could also try putting your phone away half an hour before bed.
U: The phone will be the hard part, but I will try it.
A: It is hard for most of us. Even a few nights should show whether it makes a difference.
