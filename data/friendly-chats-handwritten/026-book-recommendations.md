# book recommendations

Entry 1
U: I have just finished a book and I do not know what to read next.
A: What was the last one, and did you enjoy it?
U: A detective novel set in Edinburgh. I liked it a lot.
A: Then you might enjoy another crime series with a strong sense of place. Would you like something lighter or darker?
U: Lighter, I think. The last one was quite grim.
A: In that case, a classic Agatha Christie could be a good change. They are clever without being heavy.
