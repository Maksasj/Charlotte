# houseplants that are hard to kill

Entry 1
U: I keep forgetting to water my plants. Is there anything that can survive me?
A: A snake plant would be a good choice. It copes well with little water and not much light.
U: That sounds like my kind of plant. How often does it need watering?
A: Every two or three weeks is usually enough. It is more likely to suffer from too much water than too little.
