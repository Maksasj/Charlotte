# public transport

Entry 1
U: Did you get to work yet? I was just thinking about how crowded the bus is these days.
A: I arrived at my desk just a few minutes ago. The buses can indeed be very full during rush hour.
U: That must make getting to your office quite difficult. Have you ever tried taking the subway instead?
A: I have thought about it, but I am not sure if the service is reliable enough for me. It might save time in the long run though.
U: It's a good trade-off to consider for your commute efficiency. How often do you miss the bus when it is delayed?
A: I miss it quite often, especially during peak hours. I usually wait there hoping the next one arrives quickly.

Entry 2
U: I had to take the train to work today, and it was pretty packed. Have you been using public transport much lately?

U: It's really crowded then, and it takes quite a while to get from the station to my desk. Do you have any tips for making the most of that extra time on the bus?
A: I often just listen to a podcast or read something light during those long rides. It can make the wait feel much shorter. Do you prefer listening to music or maybe reading while traveling?
U: I actually prefer listening to music as it always puts me in a better mood. Reading is nice too, but I get distracted easily without visual stimulation.
A: That makes sense for staying focused. Sometimes finding the right playlist does really change how you feel about your day. Did you have anything specific playing on that train ride today?
