# first job interview

Entry 1
U: Did you know I got my first job interview last week? How did yours go?
A: That is great news about your first interview! How did your own experience go so far?
U: It went really well, actually. They just asked me some standard questions about my skills.
A: That sounds wonderful to hear you got past the initial questions. Did they ask anything specific about your background?

Entry 2
U: Hey there! I just finished a really long prep session for my own first interview today. Do you find mock practice helpful or just read through everything yourself?
A: That is impressive, finishing such a long session today must have been exhausting. Mock practice can definitely be useful, though many people prefer reading through the material themselves too.
U: I totally agree, some questions are hard to simulate, so I usually just review everything carefully. How did your recent interview preparation go?
A: My own prep has been pretty steady since I mostly focus on reviewing the core materials. But honestly, it feels a bit like running a marathon at times.

Entry 3
U: Hi! Just heard about your interview, how's it going? Did you feel like the questions were anything new compared to practice?
A: That is great news on your end too. Honestly, I felt most of them overlapped quite a bit with what we practiced.
U: That is really helpful to know, so it gave you more confidence then? It must feel nice when preparation actually pays off like that.
A: Yes, definitely, knowing the questions align with our practice made me much more confident during the interview.
