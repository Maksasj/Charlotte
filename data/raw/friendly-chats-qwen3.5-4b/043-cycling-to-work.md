# cycling to work

Entry 1
U: Have you ever considered cycling to work? It sounds like a nice change of pace.
A: That is a great idea and definitely healthier than driving. I will have to try it sometime soon though.
U: I think the fresh air helps too, doesn't it? Plus, you save money on gas.
A: You are absolutely right about the air and saving money as well. It really adds up when you think about all the benefits.
U: It does make sense to look at the big picture like that. Have you ever ridden early in the morning?
A: Not often, but I would love to experience a quiet street during those hours. The weather can vary quite a bit though depending on the season.

Entry 2
U: I actually started biking to work last month, and it has been quite refreshing. Do you think it is easy to maintain a safe distance while riding?
A: That sounds like a wonderful change of pace! Yes, staying alert when turning or merging with cars can definitely be tricky.
U: I've had to keep my eye on the road constantly to make sure I don't cause any trouble. It does take some getting used to though.
A: That makes sense; it is certainly a new skill to master. Many drivers are also surprised by how much slower they drive at first.
U: It really is a whole different mindset on the road, isn't it? I often find myself smiling even if I'm stuck in traffic.
A: That is quite a nice change of heart and perspective. It certainly helps to see the city from a different angle while you ride.
U: Yes, the view changes so much compared to sitting in an office all day. Sometimes I just listen to music and forget where I am going for a few blocks.
A: That is a lovely way to use the time on your bike ride. Listening to music often takes people's mind off their usual routines.

Entry 3
U: Did you know there is a new bike lane right next to our office now? It makes me wonder if many of us will start taking it for a ride.
A: That is really convenient news about the bike lane. I suppose lots of people might prefer biking since it saves time on traffic.
U: I completely agree that avoiding traffic must feel wonderful when you are heading to work each day. It has really changed my morning routine for the better.
A: Glad to hear your mornings are going smoother now. Maybe we could test the new route sometime during our break next week.
U: That sounds like a great plan. I would love to check it out with you then.
A: Perfect, that would be wonderful. Let me know when you're free so we can get started.
