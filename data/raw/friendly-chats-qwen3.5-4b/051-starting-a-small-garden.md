# starting a small garden

Entry 1
U: I was thinking I should try starting a small garden this spring. Have you given that any thought lately?
A: That sounds like a lovely idea for the upcoming season. I have been wanting to get outside more too, but haven't started yet.
U: It could be really nice just to tend to some plants and take a break from our usual routine. What kind of garden do you think would fit best in your space?
A: I think vegetable herbs like basil or mint would fit nicely here. They wouldn't take up too much room but are quite rewarding to grow.
U: That is a great choice since they are so easy to care for. Do you have any particular recipes in mind where you might use them often?
A: Yes, I love making simple salads with fresh basil or pesto for pasta. They always taste much better when grown right here instead of store-bought herbs.

Entry 2
U: That sounds like a wonderful idea for fresh produce in our city. Have you picked out your first seeds yet?
A: I haven't started planting yet, but I'm thinking about trying some heirloom varieties this spring. What kind of vegetables do you usually prefer growing?
U: I mostly grow tomatoes and herbs, though I often struggle with the tomatoes being too small. Are there any specific plants you are interested in trying first?
A: Tomatoes can definitely be tricky to get right, so basil or zucchini might make easier starts for you. Do you think you have enough space for several of them?

Entry 3
U: It really is beautiful when the sun finally warms up, don't you think? I was thinking about getting some plants to try growing myself this spring.
A: That does sound lovely. Growing your own plants could be a great way to enjoy that warm weather right away.
U: I actually haven't started yet, so I'm mostly just dreaming about it while the days are getting longer. It might be easier than expected though.
A: That sounds like a relaxing hobby to look forward to. You could start small with just a few easy varieties first.
