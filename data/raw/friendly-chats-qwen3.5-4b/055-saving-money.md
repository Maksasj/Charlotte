Entry 3

U: That helps, but sometimes it really does feel like a gamble with ingredients. Do you think having a budget would make planning easier for you?

U: You're absolutely right; seeing exactly how much is left really takes the guesswork out of it. I bet that extra clarity makes all the difference in sticking to your goals.
