# asking for a raise

Entry 1
U: Hey there! I was thinking about how hard it has been lately to juggle my workload. Have you ever considered asking for a raise if things are getting tough?
A: That is a really smart thing to consider given the situation you're in. I always think negotiating can feel quite daunting, so do you have any experience with that?
U: No, honestly I have never had to negotiate for anything before, which makes this feeling pretty overwhelming. Have you ever done it at your job or somewhere else?
A: I actually haven't tried negotiating myself, so my advice would just be to start by researching your market value. It often helps to have concrete data ready when the time comes.
U: That makes sense; having the numbers in front of me could definitely make a big difference. Do you think it's best to bring everything up at once or break it into smaller steps?
A: Breaking it down into smaller steps usually feels less intimidating and gives you time to adjust your strategy as you go. Many people find that approach much more manageable than trying to discuss everything all at once.
U: That is a really good point, and I think taking things slowly would definitely help me feel more confident. It makes sense to plan out exactly what you want before the conversation starts.
A: You are absolutely right about preparing beforehand. It often takes away a lot of the uncertainty when you know exactly what you need from the discussion.
