# coffee and tea

Entry 1
U: Did you ever try that new café downtown? I heard they make some incredible tea blends there.
A: I haven't been there yet, but the reviews sound absolutely delicious. I might check it out on my next weekend trip.
U: That sounds like a perfect idea for a relaxing weekend break. Have you tried any specific type of coffee lately?
A: I've been trying some oat milk lattes lately, but I'm still looking for the perfect cup. Do you prefer black or something creamier?
U: I actually prefer my coffee with just a splash of milk. It feels like the best balance between bold and smooth.
A: That definitely sounds like a great middle ground to me. I'll have to remember that when I finally try it out there.
U: It really does make all the difference in the taste. Maybe you should grab one from home before heading over for a quick test drive.
A: I think that's a very smart way to check them out first. You are right, testing locally saves time later.

Entry 2
U: It's been a bit chilly lately, so I've been drinking more coffee than usual to stay warm. Have you noticed if the cafes are opening their regular tea hours early?
A: That is a clever idea for keeping warm on such cool mornings. It sounds like the tea shops might be getting busy with that extra cup of energy.
U: I wonder if they also offer pumpkin spice blends since people love those when it gets cold outside. Do you think they will carry them too this season?
A: That does sound like a nice thing to offer right now in the fall. I don't know for sure, but many places do start carrying them around this time.
U: I guess so. I hope they stock decent quality ones since some of them are quite sugary. Have you tried any specific brands recently?
A: That is a fair concern about the sugar content in many blends. I have not tried specific brands myself, but I know some people prefer unsweetened options to avoid that excess.

Entry 3
U: I recently tried a small batch of artisanal coffee from a local roaster, and it was surprisingly smooth. Have you had any luck finding a good single-origin bean lately?
A: That sounds wonderful! I've been looking for something new lately but haven't found the perfect one yet. Do you know any specific places to try next?
U: I have heard great things about a roaster on 4th Street who sources beans directly from Ethiopia. You might want to check them out when you get a chance to browse their selection.
A: That is a fantastic recommendation, thank you for sharing it with me. I will definitely add them to my list of places to visit soon.
