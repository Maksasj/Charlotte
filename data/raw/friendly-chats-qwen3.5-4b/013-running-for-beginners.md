# running for beginners

Entry 1
U: I've been thinking about starting to run but I'm not sure where to begin. Have you tried running before?
A: Yes, I do enjoy running occasionally on weekends for my own fun. It is a really great way to get active though.
U: That sounds lovely, it's good to find activities that make the week feel better. Do you have any tips for beginners like me?
A: Start by going just a few times and listening to your body before pushing yourself too hard. Maybe trying short, easy runs in the beginning will help you get comfortable.
