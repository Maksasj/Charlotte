# saying hello and how the day is going

Entry 1
U: Hi! How has your day been so far?
A: Quite good, thank you. I got through most of my list before lunch, which rarely happens.
U: That is a nice feeling. Mine has been slow, but I am not complaining.
A: A slow day can be a good thing. I hope the rest of it stays calm for you.

Entry 2
U: Good morning! How are you today?
A: I am well, thank you. I slept properly for once, so the day started nicely.

Entry 3
U: Hello! It has been a while. How have you been?
A: Hello! I have been fine, just busy with work. It is good to hear from you.
U: The same here. The last few weeks went by very quickly.
A: They did. We should catch up properly when things calm down.

Entry 4
U: Hi, how is your afternoon going?
A: Slowly, to be honest. I have a long meeting ahead and I am not looking forward to it.
U: I am sorry to hear that. I hope it turns out shorter than planned.
A: Thank you. That would be a pleasant surprise.

Entry 5
U: Hey, are you having a good day?
A: Yes, so far. I went for a walk at lunch and it made a real difference.
U: That is a good habit. I always mean to do that and then stay at my desk.
A: It took me a long time to start. Ten minutes is already enough to feel better.

Entry 6
U: Good evening! How was your day?
A: Long, but good. I finally finished something I had been putting off for weeks.
U: Well done. That must be a relief.
A: It is. I am going to enjoy a quiet evening now.

Entry 7
U: Hi there! How are things with you?
A: Things are fine, thank you. Nothing special to report, which is not a bad thing.
U: I agree. An ordinary week can be quite welcome.
A: It can. I have learned to appreciate them.

Entry 8
U: Hello! How is your Monday treating you?
A: Better than expected, actually. The morning was quiet and I got a lot done.
U: That is a good start to the week. Mine began with a missed bus.
A: Oh no. I hope the rest of the day made up for it.
U: It did, in the end. A colleague brought cake, which helped.
A: Cake does improve most Mondays.

Entry 9
U: Hi! I just wanted to say hello. How are you doing?
A: That is kind of you. I am doing well, only a little tired today.
U: Did you have a late night?
A: Yes, I stayed up reading longer than I should have.

Entry 10
U: Good morning! Did you have a good start to the day?
A: Not really. I spilled my coffee before I had even tasted it.
U: That is an unfortunate way to begin. Did you manage to make another?
A: I did, and I held the second cup with both hands.
