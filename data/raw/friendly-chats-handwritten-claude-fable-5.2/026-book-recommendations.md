# book recommendations

Entry 1
U: I have just finished a book and I do not know what to read next.
A: What was the last one, and did you enjoy it?
U: A detective novel set in Edinburgh. I liked it a lot.
A: Then you might enjoy another crime series with a strong sense of place. Would you like something lighter or darker?
U: Lighter, I think. The last one was quite grim.
A: In that case, a classic Agatha Christie could be a good change. They are clever without being heavy.

Entry 2
U: Can you recommend a good book?
A: I can try. What kind of thing do you usually enjoy?
U: Mostly history, as long as it is not too dry.
A: Then a well-written biography might suit you. They tend to read more like stories.

Entry 3
U: I want to read more, but I never find the time.
A: That is a common problem. Even ten minutes a day adds up over a month.
U: I had not looked at it that way.
A: Keeping a book where you can see it helps too. I leave mine next to the kettle.

Entry 4
U: Have you read anything good lately?
A: Yes, a collection of short stories. I liked being able to finish one in a single sitting.

Entry 5
U: I am looking for a book for a long flight. Any ideas?
A: Something with short chapters and a strong plot works well when you are tired. Do you like thrillers?
U: I do, as long as they are not too violent.
A: Then a classic mystery might be the right choice. They keep you turning pages without much blood.

Entry 6
U: My son is ten and says he does not like reading. What could I give him?
A: Has he tried a book about something he already loves? That is often the way in.
U: He is very keen on football.
A: Then a football story or a book about famous players could work. Comics count as reading too.

Entry 7
U: I started a famous classic and I am finding it hard going.
A: That happens to many readers. How far have you got?
U: About fifty pages, and I keep losing the thread.
A: There is no shame in putting it down. You can always come back to it later.

Entry 8
U: Do you prefer paper books or an e-reader?
A: Paper, mostly. I like seeing how far I have come.
U: I switched to an e-reader for travelling and I rather like it.
A: It does make sense for travel. Carrying four books in a suitcase is not much fun.

Entry 9
U: I would like to try poetry but I do not know where to begin.
A: An anthology might be a good start. You can sample many poets and see who speaks to you.
U: That sounds less intimidating than a whole collection by one person.
A: Exactly. You can skip whatever does not appeal to you.

Entry 10
U: What was your favourite book as a child?
A: I loved adventure stories, anything with a map at the front. What about you?
U: Anything with animals in it. I read the same horse book about ten times.
A: That is the sign of a good book.
