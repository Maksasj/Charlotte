# weekend plans

Entry 1
U: Do you have anything planned for the weekend?
A: Nothing big. I would like to visit the market on Saturday morning and then keep the rest free.
U: That sounds pleasant. I am helping a friend move, so mine will be less relaxing.
A: That is kind of you. I hope there are not too many stairs involved.
U: Third floor and no lift, unfortunately.
A: Oh dear. In that case I hope your friend is at least buying dinner afterwards.

Entry 2
U: What are you doing this weekend?
A: I am visiting my parents on Sunday. Saturday is still open.

Entry 3
U: Any plans for Saturday?
A: I am going to a small concert in the evening. A friend of mine is playing.
U: That sounds lovely. What kind of music is it?
A: Mostly folk songs, with a guitar and a violin. It should be a relaxed evening.

Entry 4
U: I cannot wait for the weekend. Are you doing anything nice?
A: I am planning to do very little, and I am looking forward to it.
U: That sounds ideal. Sometimes the best plan is no plan.
A: I think so too. I will let you know how well I manage it.

Entry 5
U: Do you want to do something on Sunday?
A: I would like that. Did you have something in mind?
U: There is a new exhibition at the city museum. We could go in the afternoon.
A: Good idea. Shall we meet there at two?
U: Two works for me. See you at the entrance.
A: Perfect. I am looking forward to it.

Entry 6
U: How was your weekend?
A: Very nice, thank you. We went to the coast for a day and the weather held.
U: I am glad to hear it. Was the sea warm enough to swim?
A: Not for me. I only got as far as my knees.

Entry 7
U: I have a free weekend for the first time in a month.
A: That must feel good. Do you know how you will spend it?
U: I think I will finally sort out the garden. It has been neglected.
A: That is a satisfying job once it is done. I hope the weather stays dry for you.

Entry 8
U: Are you around this weekend?
A: Only on Saturday. I am travelling on Sunday for work.
U: That is a pity. Shall we have coffee on Saturday morning then?
A: Yes, gladly. The usual place at ten?

Entry 9
U: I never know what to do with my Sundays. What do you usually do?
A: I tend to cook something that takes a long time and read while it simmers.
U: That sounds very calm. I might borrow the idea.
A: Please do. A slow stew is a good place to start.

Entry 10
U: Is the weekend going to be busy for you?
A: A little. My sister is visiting with her children, so the house will be loud.
U: That sounds lively. How old are the children?
A: Four and seven. They have a lot of energy, but they are good fun.
