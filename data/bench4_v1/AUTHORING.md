# Authoring log: the Design 4.0 derivation benchmark (milestone 35, design 10.1)

All counts are in `research/PHASEB_G0.md`, generated by `tools/design4/phaseb_g0.py` from the files below. No Haiku calls were used.

## Who wrote what

- **Writers:** four Claude Code sub-agents, one per domain group. The spec is `tools/design4/BENCH_SPEC.md`.

  | Writer | Domain group |
  |---|---|
  | w1 | cooking and baking |
  | w2 | cleaning |
  | w3 | repair |
  | w4 | planning, errands, travel, crafts |

  - **Allowed sources:** the spec, design sections 4.1-4.4 and 10.1-10.3, `registry.jsonl` (symbol names and sorts from the stage 0 vocabulary) and the 3.0 procedure ground truth (step ids and roles).
  - **Never shown:** agent code, prompts, teaching, agent output or H3.
- **Reviewers:** separate Claude Code sub-agents, following `tools/design4/REVIEW_SPEC.md`. They judge each question's pieces, gold and type for world truth, one by one, and were told to disagree when unsure.
- **Checks:**
  - `tools/design4/check_bench.py` uses the independent B1-B9 reasoner of `check_fixture.py`. It checks that:
    - gold proofs derive at the stated hops;
    - GAP questions are underivable without their missing pieces and derivable with them;
    - IND golds have no base-rule proof;
    - deny holds the gold and every proof intermediate;
    - no denied atom of one question is another's piece;
    - no piece appears in more than 5% of questions;
    - there are no duplicate queries;
    - each symbol has one sort across the pool.
  - `tools/design4/complete_deny.py` adds each proof's intermediate atoms to `deny`, mechanically, and records them in `deny_added`.

## Rounds

1. **Draft.** 900 questions (`pool_v1/`), reviewed in `review/`. Writer-reviewer agreement on this draft, before any drops, is the G0 figure.
2. **Fix round 2** (`pool_v2/` is the pool after it; reviewed in `review2/`).
   - **Replaced:** questions the reviewer disagreed with, and questions where an intermediate of one proof was another question's piece. Replacements keep type, hops, scope and the near-miss flag.
   - **Renamed:** questions that used a symbol in a second sort. Registry sorts are binding. The other sense got a distinct symbol, e.g. `glass_material`, `mop_up`, `hammer_in`.
   - **Writers' own edits:** writers also changed some other questions to clear clashes with the other writers' pools. Every question whose content changed (ignoring `deny`) was reviewed again.
3. **Fix round 3** (reviewed in `review3/`).
   - The round-2 disagreements were fixed in place.
   - One more sort clash (`grease`, an action in the registry) was found by teaching every question's pieces to a stage 0 agent and reading its type-check rejections. Only the rejected pieces were passed to the writer; agent answers were not shown to writers.
   - The ambiguous `tissue_paper` was renamed `tissue_product`.

## Split and seal

`tools/design4/seal_bench.py` (seed 4035) splits the kept pool, stratified by type × hops × `kb_scope`, into `dev.jsonl` (300) and `sealed.jsonl` (600). It writes:
- `deny.txt`: the dev deny atoms and GAP missing pieces, in plain text;
- `deny_sealed.sha256`: the sealed ones, as sha256 of the canonical atom;
- `MANIFEST.sha256`.

The teacher channel's deny filter reads both, and matches sealed atoms by hash only.

## Known limits

- `kb_scope`:
  - S questions use registry symbols plus some new entities.
  - L questions need entities beyond the registry.
  - The split is about 51% S, 25% M and 24% L, against the design's target of about 40/30/30.
- Some golds rest on typical-member defaults (e.g. cookware goes in the dishwasher), with exceptions handled by EXC questions. The reviewers accepted these as true of typical cases.
- Hop counts follow the independent reasoner. The `used_for` → `can_do` bridge counts 0 hops, and so does `same_as` renaming.
