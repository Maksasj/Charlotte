## Correction (found in milestone 36): scope by the design's definition

- dev: S 3, M 2, L 295 (recomputed from registry membership of every piece symbol).
- sealed: S 13, M 1, L 586 (recomputed from registry membership of every piece symbol).

Design 10.1 defines L scope as a question that needs entities beyond the registry, with targets of about 40/30/30. The benchmark spec let writers add entities freely and label scope by domain, and no check tested scope against the registry, so the `kb_scope` line above is **wrong**: by the definition the benchmark is almost entirely L. This was not one of the G0 criteria, but it makes the benchmark unable to measure the S curriculum (dev coverage 1% after the first S run; research/M36_FINDINGS.md). The sealed part was never read. Per D4.17 the benchmark is re-authored with registry-bound scope enforced by a check, and G0 is re-reported for it.
